The work of a master’s hands

Touch, texture
and handcraft

Every piece is born from layers — layer upon layer of material, colour and texture — until the surface takes on a life of its own.

In my work I combine mixed-media techniques with texture paste, plaster, acrylic and gold leaf, creating luxurious pieces that invite the eye to wander and the hand to touch. Every detail is considered, every depth intentional — because real art must feel the way it looks.

  1. א

    Texture paste and plaster

    A rich three-dimensional texture that gives every piece depth and a tangible presence.

  2. ב

    Acrylic and pigments

    Deep, rich colours that keep their intensity for many years.

  3. ג

    Gold and silver leaf

    A luxurious touch that adds a soft gleam and elegance to any corner of the home.

  4. ד

    Mirrors and epoxy resin

    A clear finishing layer that gives the work depth, shine and a striking contemporary feel.

To devise artful designs, to work in gold, in silver and in bronze.

About the artist

Galia Adam

An artist specialising in handcrafted luxury decorative art.

My works combine textures, mirrors, crystals, natural stones, gold leaf and epoxy resin to create a unique play of light and depth. Every piece is built up in many layers and shaped with thought for the connection between art, material and emotion.

I believe every piece should be not only beautiful, but present, moving and unforgettable. Real art does not explain itself — it invites you to step inside, to touch, to breathe. Every work that leaves the studio carries layers of intention, of beauty built slowly, of a luxury you feel before you see.

The works are made in Israel for homes, offices and spaces looking for distinctive art with real presence.

The process — simple, personal and centred on you

  1. א

    Getting acquainted

    We talk about the space, the colours and how you feel when you walk in. You send a photo of the wall or the room.

  2. ב

    Concept proposal

    You receive an initial idea and direction — a sketch and a colour palette created especially for your space.

  3. ג

    Creating the work

    Every piece is made by hand, layer upon layer, until it speaks exactly the language you wanted.

  4. ד

    Delivery and hanging

    The work arrives carefully packed, ready to hang — and to change the atmosphere of your home.
